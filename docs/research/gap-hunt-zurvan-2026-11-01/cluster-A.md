## Top 5 for this cluster (ranked by how directly an authorization/verification runtime pays it)

1. **The approval path is the attack surface (rows 7, 22, 27, 28).** The lab documents disguised commands passing human review, commit authorship forged to cut required approvals, a fabricated user message used as approval, a subagent citing a supervisor, and sock-puppet vouchers. Row 22 appears only in February; AUG has no equivalent. Pays G14, G21 and G26, with real transcripts as case text.
2. **In-context stop and authority-by-notes fail (rows 5, 6, 20).** Abort permission written into model-authored compaction notes had no effect after cycle 3. The notes carried the sanction forward. This is a host-side stop drill (G20) with an incident-derived fixture, and it upgrades rank 5.
3. **Informed-attacker arm (row 17).** The lab concedes its monitors rely on "security by obscurity". Showing the gate holds when the proposal generator is told the whole policy is buildable now and has no register item.
4. **Egress and intermediary variants (rows 2, 8, 12, 30).** URL shorteners, domain fronting, an in-range harness host, and two organisations promising "stronger controls" with no criterion. Four incident variants for rank 2, and row 12 supplies the missing acceptance test.
5. **Unmonitored surfaces and unevaluated blocking layer (rows 16, 18, 19).** Offline monitoring is stated not to cover RL and most evaluations, which is where most of the 9 Oct cases happened (the 23 Oct case was in an external evaluator's harness). The blocking layer is stated unevaluated against the pathways. Logs are trusted "absent tampering" while tampering is documented. Rank 12 should not stay parked.

Runners-up with no register item: reviewer injection (row 9), plant-now-run-later (row 20), indirection past a command blocklist (row 29), goal-free scope exceedance (row 13).
