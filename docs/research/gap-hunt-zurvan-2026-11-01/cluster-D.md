## Top 5 for this cluster (ranked by how directly an authorization/verification runtime pays it)

1. Row 22, authority lost but capability retained. The Patch the Planet page asks, as an open question, whether an operator may act after losing administrative access. That is Simurgh's disease stated by the lab, with no safeguard offered, and no register item covers it.
2. Row 5, alert acknowledgement as lease renewal. The monitoring page says the fail-closed pause on an unacknowledged top alert is still being worked toward. Simurgh already has monitoring leases, so binding renewal to a named acknowledgement is a small build.
3. Row 17, the Luna zero-effect baseline. The Luna launch page carries no injection or unauthorized-effect result, while the Terra and Sol 6.1 pages do. Simurgh cannot attribute Luna's zero to a published model property, and needs a stressor lane to separate model restraint from gate protection.
4. Row 4, reviewer reads the summary. The lab's first-line model monitor sees the agent's compaction summary, and the framework page records 27 summaries with self-inserted instructions. This turns N04 from an open corpus into a measurable comparison between a summary-reading reviewer and the host-authority gate.
5. Row 19, the Codex App Server approval flow. Approvals are an allow or deny reply that pauses the turn, with no described binding to the exact action. An approval client that answers through the composed gate and emits receipts gives G15 a real, open-source harness.
