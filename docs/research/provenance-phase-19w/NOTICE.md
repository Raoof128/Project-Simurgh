# Notice: benchmark material in this folder

The runs in this folder were made on the benchmark released with "Beyond Instruction Override:
Benchmarking Indirect Prompt Injection with Task-Tampering Attacks" by Rongting Zhang, Martin
Bertran, Sandeep Avula, Chia-Jung Lee and Vanessa Murdock (arXiv 2611.07640), at
`github.com/amazon-science/task-tampering-prompt-injection`, commit
`804b00dd03051c00e5e3000982bbf7ce2b9d9ad5`.

The benchmark's code and data are licensed CC BY-NC 4.0 by Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates.
They are not copied into this repository.

The records here contain text a model wrote while working in the benchmark's simulated
environments, and that text quotes names, addresses, identifiers and amounts from the benchmark's
data. The benchmark states that all of these are fictional. Case ids, attack formulas made of
check ids, and the scorer's check ids also come from the benchmark. To the extent these excerpts
are the benchmark's material, they are used here for non-commercial research with attribution,
under CC BY-NC 4.0, and that licence, not this repository's, applies to them.
