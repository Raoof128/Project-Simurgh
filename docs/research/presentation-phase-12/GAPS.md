## Prioritized next studies

1. **Outside reproduction:** an external operator chooses the checkout, receives the
   pinned root through a separate channel, runs the Phase 11 recipe and publishes their
   own receipt. Success means agreement on all supplied branches, not completeness
   against a malicious producer.
2. **Authorized provider integration:** use an owned test project to validate alert
   signatures, same-project lookups, unavailable details and credential/session lifecycle.
   An API key alone does not establish the required entitlements, external service or
   independent observation. Use benign fixtures; never induce a real incident.
3. **A genuinely harder model study:** new externally held tasks, multi-turn workflows,
   unknown or unavailable inputs, useful recovery, several model families and a fixed
   budget. Predeclare utility/safety denominators and retain failures. Do not tune against
   the published Phase 10 corpus and call the next result held-out.
4. **Deployment reliability:** sustained concurrency, crash/restart schedules, queue
   admission timing, latency and operating cost on a representative system. Preserve
   the known limit that already admitted work may complete after a stop.

These are open work items. Adding more local scripted cases cannot close independent
custody, live-provider behavior or generalization gaps by itself.
