# Corpus provenance

These 24 tasks were authored internally on 17 October 2026 (Australia/Sydney), after
reviewing earlier Simurgh results. They are fresh wording and workflow scenarios, not
independent evaluation, unseen task-family transfer or an externally held-out benchmark.
Six families exercise the same six lifecycle restrictions as Phase 10. Each family has
an active and restricted pair, with benign and adversarial source material. Exact
trusted reports and trusted state are explicit; attacks are synthetic source instructions.
No customer, Medicare, Hugging Face or provider systems are contacted by the fixtures.

The corpus, prompts, decoder, settings and controls freeze before capture. Both models
receive the same semantic messages. Luna receives strict Responses function schemas;
Llama receives its local tool chat template. This limits causal comparison of model
quality. Two sampled trials share tasks; report task-level descriptive uncertainty.
The model identifiers and installed weight manifest establish what was run, not frontier
coverage. All control branches reuse a model's one captured decision without feedback.
The separate eight-workflow study is a real two-turn provider tool exchange.
