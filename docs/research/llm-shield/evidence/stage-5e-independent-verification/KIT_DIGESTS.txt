THE KIT IS NOT BYTE-REPRODUCIBLE, AND THAT IS A PACKAGING FACT, NOT AN EVIDENCE DEFECT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two builds of identical content produce different zip digests, because a zip stores per-file mtimes
and the builder does not normalise them. A digest here therefore pins WHICH ARTIFACT was run; it
does not let a reader rebuild that digest from source. The reproducible claim is over the extracted
CONTENT, not the archive. Anyone re-deriving these should extract and diff trees, as was done above,
rather than comparing zip digests and concluding tampering.

The Stage 5E evidence INSIDE the kit is byte-stable — that is what step 2/6 of every run checks, and
all five runs confirmed it. The archive around it is not, and no run ever claimed otherwise.
