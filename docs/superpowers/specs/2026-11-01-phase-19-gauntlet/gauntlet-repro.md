## Verdict

Do not approve as written. Two statements the design leans on are false against the repository. "No phase edits a frozen directory" fails for the credential lane, because the receiver file is pinned by six archived protocols and the audience file is pinned by the hardening report and checked in CI. Both are fixable by forking into the new namespace, but the design must say so, because that changes what the new reason can claim about Phases 8 to 17. The 5Q handling is also wrong. The re-pin has to land with the workflow under a declared P19 authority row, and splitting it into its own PR cannot go green in either order.

The bigger risk is evidence quality, not rules. The two "zero mechanism" 19a projections return constants on the existing archive (no free text for the awareness flag, and 135 of 135 effects with an intent for coverage).
