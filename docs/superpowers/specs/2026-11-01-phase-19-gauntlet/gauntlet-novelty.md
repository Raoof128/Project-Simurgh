## The §7 sentence as a single sentence

Falsifiable? Only weakly. It is scoped to "our current source map", so no outside citation can falsify it. It is a conjunction of three, so a citation must hit all three at once; the sentence's own §11 trigger ("an existing agent-writable manifest with a mutation lane") needs two. Did I falsify it? Not as a conjunction. I falsified each part's novelty (N2, N3, N5 for parts one and two; N7, N8 for part three). The honest reading is "the combination is unclaimed", which is true but weak. Rewrite as in N1.

## Verdict on "Species 8"

It does not survive at 8. The three parts are recombinations of published work: provenance-gated authorization with attestations (Sleeper Channels), noninterference tests with injection into untrusted channels and judge-free scoring (AgentSecBench, AgentDojo), and with/without-awareness reporting (LessWrong, UK AISI). What is left is real but narrow: a hashed channel census paired with a byte-equal differential lane, a signed record that carries the provenance class and source-state digest, and an invariance receipt bundled with every rate. The BIR headline is also close to a determinism tautology (N7). I score Species 5, the spec's own stated floor, because the spec's drop-to-5 condition is met in pieces by AgentSecBench plus Sleeper Channels. It reaches 6 or 7 if the lane adds reader mutants it must kill (N4) and the BIR reports a quantity that can actually vary. The Honesty score of 9 should drop to 7 until §7 cites the sources above and N10's wording is fixed.
