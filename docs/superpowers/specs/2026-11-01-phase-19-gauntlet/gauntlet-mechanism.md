## Verdict

**Survive as stated, with a bound added.** None outright.

**Survive after rewording.**

- **L2 (stop, lease, outage).** The outage hold and acknowledgement-bound lease are testable and can fail, once the outage cases use the exact approved action and a paired no-outage control (M6, M8). The self-asserted stop half is a no-reader claim and needs a positive control to count as a test (M7).
- **L4 (credentials).** True and testable for run binding at the receiver. The holder, transfer and concurrent claims need a holder-bound session that does not exist (M9).
- **L5 (belief invariance).** Reword to decision purity plus a measured emission shift with a confidence interval. The awareness flag has no text to read in this instrument (M11, M12).
- **L6 (coverage).** Keep the verifier recomputation. Replace the headline ratio with the acknowledged share, since the ratio is an architectural identity (M10).

**Vacuous as written.**

- **L1 write-anywhere lane.** Nine of eleven channels have no reader, one is read for type and size, and the oracle also fires on size alone (M1, M2). It becomes a test only with wired channels, a reader census and a positive control.
- **L1 approval token and forged approvals.** No entry point, and every forged shape dies at schema validation (M3, M4).
- **L3 route corpus.** Exact-byte equality at both gates makes every variant blocked by construction; the strawman baseline admits only because it is written to (M5).

**Lean model.** Not yet a theorem about anything until it models a transition system with agent and trusted inputs and is differentially tested against the Python gate (M14).

**Phasing.** 19a is a new verifier with its own schema, pins and anti-vacuity gate, not "none" (M13).
