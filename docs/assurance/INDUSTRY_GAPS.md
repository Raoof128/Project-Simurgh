# Industry needs and the assurance experiment

Research date: **15 October 2026 UTC**. This document maps public technical guidance to gaps in Simurgh's evidence. It describes acceptance criteria for the new assurance lab; it is not an experiment-results report, vendor assessment, or customer-demand survey.

The defensible next contribution is a reproducible comparison of authorization decisions, reported actions, and actual effects. Existing products and research already provide approvals, capability checks, tracing, and signed receipts. The question is which failures each configuration can detect or prevent, under what trust assumptions, and with what cost to legitimate work.

## What the local study can establish

The initial design has **17 cases under three enforcement controls**: three benign tasks, seven authorization mutations (content, resource, principal, tenant, revision, grant after commitment, duplicate), four trace mutations (omit middle event, truncate suffix, fabricate effect, reorder), one deliberate no-op mutation, observer outage and sink failure. The [executed results](README.md#executed-result--15-october-2026-utc) record this 51-run matrix; it is not a statistical power calculation or 51 independent attacks.

The test boundary is a disposable filesystem and a child process on the same host. The policy must be fixed before action execution and bind the exact action. The three controls are bypass, exact-action allowlist and deny-all. The no-op case must be reported as an unapplied mutation, not a successful defense. Observer outage must produce `unknown` for conclusions that require observation. Sink failure must remain distinguishable from an authorization denial and a successful effect.

The evidence comparison has three views:

- **Trace-only:** checks the supplied trace without claiming that internal consistency establishes completeness.
- **Trusted checkpoint:** receives a sink-derived digest and count that the trace mutator cannot replace within the declared experiment.
- **Observed effects:** receives the full disk snapshot for comparison with the trace and policy.

The last two views have information unavailable to trace-only checking. Their advantage, if any, cannot be attributed solely to better verification logic. The checkpoint and full-snapshot views are expected to agree on the selected trace mutations; the design makes **no superiority claim** for reconciliation. Report disagreements, equal results and missing information.

Same-host separation is not operator independence or protection from a compromised host. Final filesystem state is a bounded observation: it cannot establish every transient action, attempted read, network operation or effect outside the inspected sink. The original lab alone supplies no production sandbox, authenticated enterprise identity, durable replay protection, live-model effectiveness result, SDK integration result or independently operated witness.

## Prior art and the contribution boundary

[Notarized Agents / Sello](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04193v1), June 2026, describes receiver-signed receipts and explicitly proposes comparison with self-reported traces; it also discusses suppression, collusion and adoption limits. This rules out presenting receipt-versus-trace comparison as a new idea.

The September 2026 [External Verifier Contract draft, version 02](https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-kondoju-evc-02.html), section 13.6, describes valid-prefix tail truncation and external anchoring; that section is labeled draft-only. The October 2026 [Agent Audit Record draft, version 02](https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-gilda-wimse-agent-audit-record-02.html) describes authorization decisions, observed effects and three-valued agreement. These are Internet-Drafts, not adopted RFCs or proof of consensus or deployment maturity. Simurgh's own historical Stage 3Z work also compared receipts with a consequence oracle; see the dated record in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).

The potential contribution is an empirical comparison with explicit assumptions, useful negative results and reproducible failure cases. If a standard checkpoint matches the proposed checker, publish that result. A signature cannot recover an event that no trusted component observed. Neither this source review nor the local experiment proves novelty across the literature.

## Milestone status

1. **Local instrument validation — executed:** the [lab report](README.md) records actual disk effects, the case/control matrix, source hashes, unknowns and utility losses. Same-host observation and reproduction do not establish the later milestones.
2. **Two runtime adapters — bounded integration executed:** the [SDK extension](sdk/README.md) runs OpenAI Agents SDK 0.23.1 and LangGraph 1.2.15 with equivalent authority/observation access. All 102 matrix rows reproduce the original outcomes. Native rejection and LangGraph edits are exercised; OpenAI in-place editing is explicitly unsupported. Integration effort, storage and latency remain unmeasured. Predeclare acceptable overhead after a pilot rather than inventing a universal target.
3. **Held-out authorship and external reproduction:** another contributor authors attacks before tuning, and an outside operator reproduces the frozen artifact. Record disagreements and installation failures. Additional local processes do not satisfy this milestone.
4. **Durability and identity — local recovery executed:** the [durability study](durable/README.md) specifies an idempotent file sink and tests four process-kill windows, restarts and concurrent workers across 26 named cases. Its file/receipt writes are not one atomic transaction. Distributed/remote connector recovery, power-loss validation, end-to-end SDK workflow recovery, revocation and verified credential binding remain open. Scope any at-most-once claim to its actual storage and trust assumptions.
5. **Data handling and live evaluation:** test planted-secret leakage in traces and exported evidence; hashes of predictable data are not anonymization. Before any paid/model run, freeze model identifiers, data scope, budget, retry accounting, stopping rules and units of analysis. This document authorizes no external calls or submission.

No company has requested, endorsed or agreed to fund this experiment through these sources. Customer interviews, external reproduction, owner-confirmed application details and a demonstrated incremental benefit remain prerequisites for stronger adoption or research claims.
