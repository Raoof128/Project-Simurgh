# SimurghForge knowledge wiki

This is the Zurvan-federated wiki scaffold, not a populated research knowledge base.
`wiki/decisions/` and `wiki/claims/` are intended destinations and are absent from this checkout.
No claim or decision is considered reviewed merely because a directory is planned.

Use the [documentation map](../DOCUMENTATION.md), [reviewer guide](../docs/REVIEWER_INDEX.md)
and [current roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) for the available record. The [wiki log](log.md)
records scaffold activity; the root [change log](../CHANGELOG.md) records project work.
